# Calendar

With a `cal-private` or `cal-business` URL param, a pill in the top-left corner swaps the weather dashboard for a calendar. A switcher shows this month, the 7 days from today, or today alone. Each param is the URL of one Google account's feed (`apps-script/Code.gs` serves it). With both, a selector shows Private, Business, or All, each in its account's colour. The page keeps the last good feed per account in localStorage, so it shows data offline. It also remembers the view, the selection and the span. Without a `cal-` param, the page is the plain weather dashboard.

## Sub-features

- `cal-absent`: no `cal-` param gives no pill, no calendar, and no feed request, even when the saved view is `calendar`.
- `cal-toggle`: the pill reads `Calendar` on the dashboard and `Weather` on the calendar, and a tap swaps the views.
- `cal-select`: **Private**, **Business**, and **All** each show only their events. The selected option is filled with its colour.
- `cal-single`: with one param, the calendar has no selector.
- `cal-persist`: the view, the selection and the span survive a reload (localStorage `calendar-view-v1`).
- `cal-span`: **Month**, **Week**, and **Day** sit left of the selector, also with one account. The chosen one is filled white. Week is the default, and an unknown saved span falls back to it. A tap re-renders from the events already fetched, with no feed request, because one fetch covers all three spans (from the Monday on or before the 1st of the month, to the later of the month's last Sunday and today+6).
- `cal-days`: the week span. 7 columns `Today`, `Tomorrow`, then a short weekday and day (`Mon 23`). All-day events come first as filled chips, then timed events as `HH:MM title` with a bar. Chips and bars take the event's `color` from the feed (its Google calendar's colour), or the account colour when the feed has none. An event that runs past midnight shows `to HH:MM` on its last day. An empty day shows `Free`. A full column fades out at the bottom. Titles render as text, never as HTML.
- `cal-month`: a Monday-first grid of the weeks that hold this month (4 to 6 rows filling the height) under a row of short weekday names. Each cell has its day number, then the events that fit, then `+N more`. Past days and days outside the month are dimmed (`.dim`), and today is outlined in the accent colour (`.today`). An all-day event shows in every day it covers.
- `cal-day-view`: today only. All-day events sit as chips in a row at the top. Below, a 24-hour timeline (`.cal-scroll`, 7.5vh per hour) scrolls up and down. It opens half an hour above the next event that has not ended (on today) or the first event, else at 07:30, so `08:00` is the first full hour. A refresh re-renders it and scrolls back. Timed events are blocks placed by time with `HH:MM–HH:MM title` and the location under it when it fits. Overlapping events split the width into columns. A day with no events shows `Free`.
- `cal-day-preview`: in the month span, a tap anywhere in a cell, events included, opens a dialog with that day's date, **Close**, and the day view of that date (all-day chips, then the hour timeline). A tap on an event in it opens `cal-details` on top. Closing the details leaves the day open. Both dialogs share one idle timer, so a minute with no tap or scroll closes both.
- `cal-details`: a tap on an event in the week or day span, or in the month span's day dialog, opens a dialog with its title, date and time, account, location, and description as plain text. The bar takes the event's colour and the account dot the account colour. A tap on the backdrop or **Close** closes it, and so does a minute with no scrolling.
- `cal-zone`: day columns and times follow the weather location's timezone, like the clock.
- `cal-offline`: with every feed unreachable, the cached events stay and `#cal-status` reads `Offline · showing HH:MM data` with class `error`.
- `cal-account-fail`: when one feed fails, the other account still updates. `#cal-status` names the failed account: `Business calendar failed · showing HH:MM data` with a cache, or `Business calendar failed` without one. A malformed feed counts as a failure.
- `cal-retry`: after any failed fetch the page fetches every feed again 60 s later. After a success it waits 10 min.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Point the frame at a URL with `cal-private=` and `cal-business=` (URL-encoded `/exec` URLs), then tap **Calendar**.
- Tap **Private**, **Business**, or **All** at the top right of the calendar.
- Tap **Month**, **Week**, or **Day** left of that.
- Lose Wi-Fi, or reboot the frame without it.

## Driving it with cdp.mjs

Preconditions: baseline. Generate the fixture feeds first, because their events are dated from today in Europe/Warsaw. `fixtures/make.mjs` writes `private.json`, `business.json` (with 18 events on day 5 to overflow its column), and `malformed.json`, and `bad-color.json` (a colour that is not `#rrggbb`). In `private.json`, `Gym` and `Mum's birthday` are `#33b679`, `Ski trip` is `#8e24aa`, and the rest have no colour. For the month and day spans, `private.json` also has `Haircut` 5 days ago and `Book fair` all day from yesterday to day 2, and `business.json` has `Offsite` all day 12 and 11 days ago, plus `Budget review` (14:00–15:30, in `Room 4.12`) and `Vendor call` (14:30–15:00) today, which overlap. The dev server serves the files as they are, ignoring `from` and `days`. Below, day 1 is the `Today` column.

```sh
node .claude/skills/verify-frame-app/fixtures/make.mjs

ST="[document.getElementById('cal-status').textContent, document.getElementById('cal-status').className]"
```

## Gotchas

- The fixtures are dated when `make.mjs` runs. Run it again after midnight in Warsaw.
- The past fixture events are dated back from today, so in the first days of a month some land in the month before, dimmed or off the grid.
- The `+N more` counts depend on the font and the viewport. At 1280x800, day 5 reads `+18 more`.
- The month cells measure their events to fit them, which needs the calendar on screen. A tap on `#view-toggle` re-renders, so it fits then.
- An Apps Script deployment older than `from` ignores it and serves 14 days from today. The fixtures, served as static files, stand in for that: they ignore `from` and `days` too.
- The fixture times are Warsaw wall-clock times, so another `lat`/`lon` moves them. The timezone recipe relies on that.
- `#view-toggle` flips the saved view. Read `document.body.dataset.view` before you tap it, because a reload keeps the last view.
- A tap on the pill or the selector also refreshes the weather, through the page's tap-anywhere listener. A tap never refreshes the calendar.
- The dev server answers a missing fixture path with `index.html` and status 200. The page rejects that as a malformed feed.
- The cache entry for an account is used only when its URL matches, so pointing `cal-business` at a new URL starts it with no cache.
- The calendar's move to a new day at midnight cannot be driven without faking the clock. Report it unproven.
